FAQ

Common questions

Can't find your answer? Ask us directly on WhatsApp, free and with no obligation.

How much does it cost?

The Profile & Catalogue package starts from IDR 1.5 million. For our first 3 clients, we offer an introductory price of IDR 750,000, provided we may show the project as an example of our work. The Supply-Ready Page and English version are priced separately. We send a detailed written quote, with no hidden costs.

Are there monthly fees?

Not required. If you want your website kept up to date, we offer Basic Care at IDR 150,000 per month (regular checks and 2 small changes) and Active Care at IDR 350,000 per month (up to 8 changes, including new products and prices).

Will the website bring in buyers on its own?

Honestly: not automatically. A website makes you trusted when buyers check your business, or when you offer your products to them. Once your website is ready, we give you sample messages for approaching hotels, restaurants, and shops with your website link.

How long until the website is live?

It depends on how complete your materials are, such as photos and product details. We give you a time estimate at the start, after the consultation.

I'm not good with technology. Can I still do this?

Yes. You only need to prepare photos and business information. We take care of the technical side, and you get a simple guide once the website is live.

Do product photos need a professional photographer?

Not necessarily. Bright, tidy photos from a phone are enough. We give you a short guide on how to photograph your products so they look convincing.

I'm outside Bali. Can you still help?

Yes. The whole process runs via WhatsApp and email, so we can work with producers anywhere in Indonesia.

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain is registered in your business's name, and all website files are handed over to you.
